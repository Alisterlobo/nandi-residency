About Nandi Residency

Welcome to Nandi Residency, Dharwad Your perfect urban getaway in the heart of Dharwad, built around simple things done well: clean rooms, honest service and a front desk that picks up the phone. Guests stay with us for work, hospital visits, exams, weddings and family trips.

Nandi Residency is a clean and comfortable lodge in Dharwad, on Haliyal Road near Kud Circle. Guests choose us for AC rooms, a friendly front desk and an easy location, 2.7 km from Dharwad railway station.
